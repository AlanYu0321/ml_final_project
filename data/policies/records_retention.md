# Records Retention Policy
Tags: retention, records, legal-hold, deletion, audit

## Business records
Business records must be stored in approved systems with access logging. Local copies should be deleted when the business purpose ends.

## Legal holds
Legal holds override standard deletion timelines. Employees must not delete records subject to a legal hold until legal releases the hold.

## Audit logs
Audit logs for production systems must be retained for at least one year. Security investigation logs should be retained with the incident record.

## Deletion requests
Deletion requests must identify the system, record type, owner, and retention basis. If unsure, escalate to legal before deleting.
