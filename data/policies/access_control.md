# Access Control Policy
Tags: access, identity, permissions, least-privilege, audit

## Least privilege
Access must be limited to the minimum permissions needed for the employee's current role. Managers must review privileged access every 90 days.

## Access requests
New access requests require a business justification, manager approval, and system owner approval before provisioning. Emergency access expires within 24 hours unless the security team approves an extension.

## Shared accounts
Shared accounts are prohibited for production systems. Service accounts must have an owner, a rotation schedule, and audit logging enabled.

## Offboarding
Employee access must be removed within 24 hours of termination. Contractor access must have an end date and must be reviewed monthly.
