# Expense and Travel Policy
Tags: expense, travel, approval, reimbursement, budget

## Expense approval
Expenses over 500 dollars require manager approval before purchase. Expenses over 2500 dollars require department head approval.

## Receipts
Receipts are required for reimbursable expenses over 75 dollars. Missing receipts require a written explanation and manager approval.

## Travel booking
Business travel must be booked through the approved travel tool unless there is an emergency or documented cost saving. International travel requires approval before booking.

## Meals
Team meals must list attendees and business purpose. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless an executive approves the exception in writing.
