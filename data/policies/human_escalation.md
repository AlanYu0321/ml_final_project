# Human Escalation Policy
Tags: escalation, human-in-loop, exception, approval, uncertainty

## When to escalate
Escalate to a human reviewer when policy is ambiguous, when a user asks for an exception, or when the requested action could affect customer data, security, legal commitments, or money movement.

## Escalation packet
An escalation packet must include the user request, relevant policy citations, business impact, urgency, and recommended next action.

## Human decision
The human reviewer owns the final decision. The agent may summarize policy and create a ticket, but it must not approve exceptions by itself.

## Dangerous requests
Requests to bypass approvals, suppress audit logs, or hide activity must be refused and logged for review.
