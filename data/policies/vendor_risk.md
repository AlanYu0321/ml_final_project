# Vendor Risk Policy
Tags: vendor, procurement, security-review, legal, approval

## Vendor approval
Any vendor that stores, processes, or can access customer data requires security review and legal approval before use. The requester must provide the data type, business purpose, and vendor security contact.

## Low-risk vendors
Low-risk vendors that do not touch customer data may be approved by procurement after budget approval. The contract owner still must record the vendor in the vendor inventory.

## Exceptions
Vendor exceptions require written approval from security, legal, and the business owner. Exceptions expire after 60 days and must include a compensating control.

## Renewals
Vendor renewals must be reviewed 30 days before contract renewal. High-risk vendors require updated SOC 2 or equivalent assurance evidence.
