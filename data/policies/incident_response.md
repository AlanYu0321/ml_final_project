# Incident Response Policy
Tags: incident, escalation, security, severity, logging

## Reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within one hour. Include timeline, affected systems, data involved, and current mitigation steps.

## Severity
Potential exposure of customer data is at least severity 2. Active exploitation, credential theft, or public disclosure is severity 1.

## Containment
Teams may isolate affected systems to stop active harm, but destructive remediation requires incident commander approval. Preserve logs and evidence before cleanup.

## Communications
External communications about incidents must be approved by legal and communications. Do not notify customers until the incident commander approves the message.
