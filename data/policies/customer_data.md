# Customer Data Handling Policy
Tags: pii, customer-data, privacy, retention, encryption

## Customer PII
Customer PII may not be pasted into chat tools, tickets, or vendor portals unless the system is approved for regulated data. Mask PII before sharing examples.

## Encryption
Customer data must be encrypted in transit and at rest. Exported customer records must be stored in approved company storage with access logging.

## Retention
Raw customer exports must be deleted within 30 days unless a legal hold or approved retention exception applies. Aggregated metrics may be retained for analytics if they cannot identify a customer.

## Data subject requests
Privacy requests must be routed to the privacy queue within one business day. Do not promise deletion timelines without privacy team confirmation.
